Week 1 — Psychology of a Scam 🧠

How scammers use urgency, fear and authority to bypass judgment.

Main message

“Before you react, recognise the manipulation.”

Teach employees to notice emotional pressure before they click, reply or transfer information.

Week 2 — Spot the Fake 👁️

Deepfakes, voice cloning and malicious social media ads.

Main message

“Seeing or hearing it doesn't automatically make it real.”

Move employees from passive awareness to active verification.

Week 3 — The QR Code Trap 📱

Awareness around “quishing” and unexpected malicious links.

Main message

“A QR code is a doorway — check where it leads.”

Introduce the QR + phishing idea and teach employees to pause before scanning unexpected codes.

Week 4 — Verifying the Source 🔎

Out-of-band verification for urgent requests, especially finance and HR.

Main message

“Urgent does not mean legitimate.”

Teach employees to verify important requests through another trusted channel before acting.
